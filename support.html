Common Questions

How do I delete my account and data?

In PauseWell, go to Me → Profile, scroll down, and select “Delete my account & data.” Follow the confirmation steps shown in the app.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open PauseWell and go to the subscription area under Me. Select “Restore purchases” while signed in with the Apple ID originally used for the purchase.

Why isn't Apple Health data appearing?

Apple Health features require permission to access supported health information. You can review the permissions granted to PauseWell through Apple Health and your device privacy settings. Available information will also depend on the data recorded on your device.

How does voice symptom logging work?

PauseWell requires microphone access when you choose to log symptoms by voice. Speak naturally about what you're experiencing and review your entry before continuing.

Where is my Doctor Report?

Go to Me → History → Doctor Report. From there you can generate your longitudinal wellness report and save or share the PDF.

Why don't I see insights yet?

Some insights require enough logged history to identify a meaningful pattern. Continue logging your experiences over time as your personal history develops.
